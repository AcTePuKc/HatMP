[B]Unofficial Hermaphrodity 0.28.31 - AI Assisted Bulgarian & European Spanish[/B]

Based on the official 0.28.3 release. This is localization build, now available for players to try.

[B]What's new[/B]
• Bulgarian and European Spanish translations for the story, choices, phone, map, character schedules, and much of the interface. You can change the language from the title screen or phone settings.
• A new Enhanced dialogue view alongside Classic, with text size, spacing, and background settings. Recent dialogue history is available during the current session.
• Continue on the title screen, automatic return to the current room after a page refresh, and Phone → Settings → Main Menu.
• Better wording for the player's selected gender and customizable names. Additional relationship forms help Bulgarian and Spanish sentences sound natural.
• A small glossary, inline translations for Zoey's French phrases, and translated labels on many action buttons.
• Interface and scene fixes, including the Naked Beaver interaction after the cult letter, several dialogue routes, and some overlapping controls.
