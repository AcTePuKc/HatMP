## How to use this log

- Compare the old base, our version and the newer version for each affected scene. Keep new upstream content and fixes while carrying forward applicable translation keys, values, player-gender forms, customizable names and localized UI.
- A room number, chat ID, callback or English sentence can change between versions. Match by scene and behavior before reusing a translation; do not assume an identical key still means the same thing.
- Record the outcome, upstream evidence, any conflict resolution and remaining in-game checks here. Entries without a decision are open questions, not approved fixes.
- Keep this log updated as new localization changes, gameplay fixes or uncertain upstream differences are discovered. The detailed room coverage and wording decisions remain in [README.md](README.md).
- The user expects that the developer may already have fixed some of these issues in the newer version. This has not yet been verified: compare the actual upstream implementation before carrying over any local fix.

## Known compatibility note: relationship forms in old saves

Older saves with custom relationship names can lack optional grammatical forms. Standard default names already have localized forms; custom names fall back to their base form, which may sound ungrammatical in Bulgarian and Spanish. Players can review and save forms in Phone → Characters/Relationships → relationship terms. Bulgarian suggestions appear only while editing a supported base word and should be checked; saved or manually edited forms are preserved. No form is generated merely by loading a save. Custom terms and forms are shared across languages, so a language change may require another review. The current English catalog does not contain the extra-form placeholders searched in this audit; do not assume all English contexts or future languages are immune. Check each new locale and its actual dialogue during QA and the version transition. See [README.md](README.md) for the detailed behavior.
