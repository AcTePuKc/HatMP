## Validation and next steps

The complementary static candidate scanner runs with `npm run l10n:scan --prefix tools`; `-- --path scripts/rape.js` restricts the candidate scan to one source file. Add `--locale bg,es,it,de,ru,fr` to compare chosen languages, including packs that do not yet exist, and `--en-proposals` to export a separate review-only JSON of statically known source strings absent from `en.js`. `--out` paths are relative to the project root. The English proposals do not modify `en.js`: check runtime fallbacks, placeholders, context and any duplicate keys before importing. The scanner includes keys found only in installed locale packs even when their English source is dynamic or unknown. Exact source-text matches and existing translations are suggestions, not proof that another occurrence is wired to that key; equal-to-source can be a valid translation. Candidate counts are not a definitive count: text may be localized indirectly, be unreachable, or be overwritten at runtime; assembled text is marked for review, and indirect/data-driven paths are not exhaustively traced. Latest combined report and English proposals: `tools/reports/unlocalized-coverage-2026-11-12.md`, `.json`, `.en-proposals.json`. Regenerate after edits rather than treating counts as current.

Continue auditing shared systems and dynamic dialogue, then rerun in-game QA in BG/ES/EN. After transition to the newer official version, compare the recorded changes and outstanding issues with upstream. Avoid treating source defaults in catalogs, comparison strings, asset names, comments or already-localized data as evidence of missing translations.
